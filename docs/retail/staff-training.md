# Store team quick start

## Practice first

Open `/retail/demo`. Every store, customer and result is fictional. Choose a customer, complete the three practice steps, publish the sample plan, and switch to Member view. Add a demo reply and compare the sample store results. Reset returns to the starting state; closing the tab discards all practice changes. Nothing is sent, charged or saved to a customer account.

The demo is a shortened rehearsal. The live consultation includes intake, measurements, private observations, customer guidance, follow-up dates and a publication preview.

## Prepare a store

1. A MacroStack superadmin provisions the organization/store and confirms its timezone and agreed sponsorship term.
2. Invite a manager and the relevant specialists. Give organization/operator access only where needed. Corporate reporting does not automatically grant access to individual customer records.
3. In Library, open an editable draft outline. Review the intake questions and guidance with the store lead, adapt the wording, then deliberately publish it. These are MacroStack starting points, not approved 5 Star material.
4. Confirm approved branding/forms and complete the partner readiness fields. Billing requires the authorized contact; the default is $599 USD per store per month.
5. In Store → Operations health, check provider and scheduler configuration. “Configured” does not replace a successful provider test. Run the consenting pilot workflow before onboarding customers.

## Serve a customer

- **Connect:** prepare the customer record and share its private invitation with the intended person. The customer signs in and chooses sharing/reminder preferences.
- **Listen:** review the intake and ask what outcome matters to them. Do not infer that absent logs mean zero intake.
- **Personalize:** use the six-step live consultation. Confirm units/dates for manual assessments or CSV imports. Keep private observations separate from the customer’s plan.
- **Review:** preview the exact customer-facing plan, agree follow-up dates, then publish. Corrections are a new plan version; history stays intact.
- **Follow through:** use Today’s paginated queue, assign conversations, and complete/reschedule actions with a note. New customer replies reopen resolved conversations.
- **Review progress:** use History for date-filtered records and exports. Export only what is needed and handle downloaded customer records according to your organization’s policies.

## When something fails

- Keep a failed draft open and retry after reconnecting. Verify the saved indicator before leaving.
- A reminder marked **accepted** has reached the provider, not necessarily the customer.
- **Unknown** means the outcome is uncertain. Review provider status; do not blindly send it again.
- **Queue retry** is available only to authorized managers for eligible temporary email failures within the idempotency window. It rechecks current consent and does not bypass quiet hours.
- Contact corrections, opt-outs, failed texts and old reminders require review. Ask the customer to update their own verified contact preferences when appropriate.
- If the worker is stale or failed, check service configuration. Do not create duplicate manual reminders to hide a delivery issue.

## Launch acceptance

Use consenting test accounts to exercise sign-in, invitation claim, intake, consultation publication, shared file upload/download, member reply, check-in, reminders/opt-out and billing in provider test mode. Confirm unrelated staff cannot see the record. Demo and fixture browser tests do not establish that external providers are configured.
