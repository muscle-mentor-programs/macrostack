Where workout-first platforms fall short

  • Nutrition is an add-on. Macro targets buried three menus deep, synced from a third-party tracker your client also has to install (often MyFitnessPal — with its paywalled scanner).
  • Check-ins are DIY. You're building forms in Google Docs and chasing clients over text.
  • No adherence visibility. You find out a client stopped logging when they ghost the monthly call — not the day it happened.
  • Per-client pricing punishes growth. Costs climb fast right as your roster grows.
